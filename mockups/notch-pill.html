Versus what's built now

  • Solid black, not frosted. Today's compact pill is a hudWindow blur with a hairline. The notch look only works if the pill is the same black as the notch, so it reads as one shape.
  • Inside the notch, not below it. Today the pill docks top-center just under the menu bar. New: the window starts at y=0 and overlaps the notch, so idle is invisible inside it and the bigger states hang out of it.
  • Always there. The idle outline stays on screen between takes, so hover works. Today the panel hides when you're not recording.
  • Hover bar replaces the chevron. Settings, record, and expand live on the bar. The full 428×120 card still exists, expand opens it.
  • Stop is the red button on the left of the recording pill, not a footer word.
  • Coral is the only color. Everything else is black, white, and one hairline.
